## Message pages

Each message has one page covering both directions:

- **Used by** shows the icon of every game that sends or receives the message. Hover over an icon for the game's name.
- **Client → Server** and **Server → Client** list the fields in the order they're sent. A section labelled "For each …" repeats.
- **Remarks** explain behaviour that the field lists can't show.
- **Appears in** links to the connection sequences that use the message.
- **Sources and credits** say where the facts came from, and credit the BNETDocs authors who documented the message first.

## Confidence

Every field, value and sequence step carries a mark:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Verified | Confirmed against a real client, or by two independent sources. |
| ⚠️ Single source | From one source only, or worked out rather than captured. Treat with care. |
| 🛑 Unknown | Not known. Don't guess in code; capture it. |

When you confirm something marked ⚠️ or 🛑, please update the page and say how you confirmed it.

## What this site never hosts

This site documents protocols. It never hosts or links to game files, version-check (hash) files, CD keys, client modifications, or ways around anti-cheat. Pages explain how those things work, not where to get them.

## Sources and credit

Pages are written in our own words. When research came from BNETDocs or another project, the page links to it and names the people behind it. Nothing is copied. See [Credits](/credits/).
